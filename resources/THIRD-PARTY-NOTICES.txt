NSGo v0.2.0
============

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, reproduced below.

NSGo
Copyright 2026 ONEO <https://oneo.me>

NSGo is a Go port of Native Shell (NSH), https://github.com/longbridge/nsh,
Copyright 2026 Longbridge <https://longbridge.com>, licensed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0. NSGo is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Longbridge.

The Go packages ported from NSH are modified: Rust is translated into Go, names
follow Go conventions, and behavior differs where ARCHITECTURE.md records it.
ARCHITECTURE.md's "Code map" names the NSH crate each package comes from.

internal/platform/objcblock follows purego's Objective-C block bridge,
https://github.com/ebitengine/purego, Copyright 2025 The Ebitengine Authors,
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. NSGo pins the templates,
keeps descriptors in native memory and checks allocation before returning a block.

core/tailwind_palette.go is Tailwind CSS's default palette,
https://github.com/tailwindlabs/tailwindcss, Copyright (c) Tailwind Labs, Inc.,
licensed under the MIT License, converted to sRGB as NSH converts it.

base/richeditor ports ProseMirror, https://prosemirror.net, Copyright (c) 2015-2017
by Marijn Haverbeke and others, and follows Tiptap, https://tiptap.dev, Copyright
(c) Tiptap GmbH, both licensed under the MIT License: their structures, algorithms,
names and command semantics apply to what it ports.

The Lucide icon set in icons/lucide comes from
https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide, Copyright (c) 2026 Lucide Icons and
Contributors, licensed under the ISC License, together with Feather's MIT
notice. icons/lucide/LICENSE holds both texts.

Layout test fixtures in internal/layout/testdata/ come from Taffy,
https://github.com/DioxusLabs/taffy, Copyright (c) 2018 Visly Inc. and
Copyright (c) 2026 Taffy Authors, licensed under the MIT License. That directory's
LICENSE file holds the full text.

The fonts in internal/woff/testdata/ are Pacifico,
https://github.com/googlefonts/Pacifico, Copyright 2018 The Pacifico Project
Authors, licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. That
directory's OFL.txt file holds the full text.

The shader compiler in internal/shader and internal/shaderir is Ebitengine's
Kage compiler, https://github.com/hajimehoshi/ebiten, Copyright 2020 The Ebiten
Authors, licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, as MyGo,
https://github.com/egoist/mygo, Copyright (c) 2026 MyGo contributors, forked
it under the MIT License. Its files keep their notices and say what MyGo
changed; NSGo replaced MyGo's bridge. internal/shader/LICENSE holds both texts.

base/formula/fonts/NewCMMath-Book.otf is New Computer Modern Math Book, Copyright (C) 2019-2026 Antonis Tsolomitis, copied unmodified from typst/typst-assets, https://github.com/typst/typst-assets, at commit 94dcb990. It is one file of the NewComputerModern package, whose complete and unmodified Work is on CTAN, https://ctan.org/pkg/newcomputermodern, version 8.1.1 of 2026-06-22. It is licensed under the GUST Font License 1.0, that is, under the LaTeX Project Public License 1.3c; base/formula/fonts/LICENSE holds both texts.

The date and time tables in internal/cldr/tables.go are generated from the
Unicode Common Locale Data Repository (CLDR), https://cldr.unicode.org,
Copyright (c) 1991-2025 Unicode, Inc., licensed under the Unicode License v3.

cmd/nsgo/dsstore.go, which lays out disk images' Finder windows, comes from
MyGo, https://github.com/egoist/mygo, Copyright (c) 2026 MyGo contributors,
licensed under the MIT License. cmd/nsgo/install follows NSH's install scripts.

base/terminal/internal/vt, base/terminal/internal/pty and
base/terminal/mirror.go and mirror_images.go come from
https://github.com/egoist/mygo, Copyright (c) 2026 MyGo contributors, licensed
under the MIT License. base/terminal/internal/library/lib holds libghostty-vt
built from Ghostty, https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty, Copyright (c) 2024
Mitchell Hashimoto, Ghostty contributors, licensed under the MIT License.
base/terminal/ghostty_themes.go holds iTerm2-Color-Schemes' themes as Ghostty
ships them, under the MIT License its header gives.

base/diagram/paint.go draws limu's scenes as limumygo, limu's adapter to MyGo, draws them, https://github.com/firede/limu, Copyright (c) 2026 Firede, licensed under the MIT License: what each item's parts, markers and label are painted in, and where a label's lines and spans go. limu, which base/diagram imports, ports much of Pele, https://github.com/obsidianmd/pele, Copyright (c) 2026 kepano, under the MIT License, and carries both notices in its LICENSE.

core/audit.go's layout audit follows mygo-layout-audit, https://github.com/knothhe/mygo-layout-audit, Copyright (c) 2026 knothhe, licensed under the MIT License: its colors, its grid and its ruler.

internal/platform/macos/permissionguide.go and permissionguide_layout.go, macOS's permission guide, port MyGo's permissionflow plugin, https://github.com/egoist/mygo, Copyright (c) 2026 MyGo contributors, licensed under the MIT License, which follows PermissionFlow, https://github.com/jaywcjlove/PermissionFlow, Copyright (c) 2026 小弟调调: what the guide does, the panes it opens and which of them take a drag, the pasteboard types of the dragged app, and the panel's geometry and text.
